# 14 COMMUNITY INTELLIGENCE — GENESIS

GENESIS is world intelligence.

## Understand
People, communities, organizations, places, services, resources and physical systems.

## Reason
Relationships, context, state, events, patterns, anomalies, needs, risks and opportunities.

## Propose
Forecasting, impact analysis, scenario analysis, proposal generation, prioritization and explanation.

## Govern
Human review, measurement, outcome analysis, provenance awareness, uncertainty awareness and truth-state awareness.

GENESIS may observe, understand, contextualize, detect, reason, measure and propose.

GENESIS may not self-authorize, override policy, invent providers, invent live state, rewrite history or execute unauthorized consequential actions.
