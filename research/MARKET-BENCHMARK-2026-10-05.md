## What LegaKeys should learn — and what it should reject

### Learn
- Apple: coherent consumer experience and device-to-service continuity.
- Microsoft/Amazon/Google/Oracle: infrastructure, identity, developer platforms and enterprise operating models.
- ServiceNow/Salesforce: systems of record + workflow + authorization.
- Stripe/Visa/Mastercard/PayPal: payment intent, authorization, execution and reconciliation separation.
- Grab/Sea: multi-service ecosystems and regional operating complexity.
- Singapore operators: integration discipline, smart-city thinking and regulated infrastructure.
- Samsung/LG/Hyundai/Naver/Kakao: device, mobility, identity and daily-life convergence.
- Huawei/Tencent/Alibaba/Baidu/DeepSeek: platform depth, infrastructure and AI systems.
- France's Schneider/Thales/Dassault/Orange/Capgemini: industrial systems, security, infrastructure and enterprise transformation.

### Reject
- surveillance as the product;
- opaque social scoring;
- authentication treated as authorization;
- community lock-in;
- invented provider availability;
- AI self-authorization;
- uncontrolled data aggregation;
- fragmented identities across services;
- dark patterns and misleading availability;
- treating a community dashboard as ownership of the platform.

## LegaKeys architectural synthesis

The strongest common pattern is not “one giant app.” It is a governed system of record and execution:
ENTITY → IDENTITY → PARTICIPATION → CONTEXT → CAPABILITY → AUTHORITY → AUTHORIZATION → ACTION → EVENT → EVIDENCE → OUTCOME.

LegaKeys adds the missing cross-layer distinction: the platform can coordinate many services while communities remain independent scopes and people retain one persistent identity.
