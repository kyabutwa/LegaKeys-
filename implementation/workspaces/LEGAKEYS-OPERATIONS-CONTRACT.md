# LegaKeys Operations Workspace — Production Contract

## Mission

Operate LegaKeys itself as the platform authority boundary without confusing platform operation with community operation.

## Surface

Product, Engineering, Identity, Access, Services, Community Operations, Intelligence, World/Digital Twin, Infrastructure/Security, Governance/Compliance, Finance, Research, Design and Partnerships.

## Boundary

LegaKeys Operations may govern platform policy and platform-owned services according to the canonical authority model. It does not silently become a community's local operational authority.

## Core controls

- canonical account/session lifecycle;
- BeatIdentity and participation boundaries;
- service capability and provider truth;
- workspace lifecycle and scoped roles;
- authorization decision lineage;
- deployment/runtime health;
- evidence and audit;
- governance and compliance;
- provider/integration boundaries.

## Operating loop

Observe → Diagnose → Prepare → Review → Authorize → Execute → Event → Evidence → Outcome → Learn.

## AI boundary

GENESIS and CONSTANTYNA remain advisory/intelligence layers. They can surface findings and prepare proposals but cannot bypass authorization.

**NO AUTHORIZATION → NO CONSEQUENTIAL ACTION.**
