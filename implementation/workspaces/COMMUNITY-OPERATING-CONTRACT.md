# Community Operating Workspace — Production Contract

## Mission

Operate an authorized participating community as a governed operational context.

## Surface

People, participation, teams, responsibilities, shifts, schedules, places, facilities, work, requests, maintenance, cleaning, security, visitors, deliveries, community governance, declared services, access operations, evidence and operational intelligence.

## Boundary

A community workspace may coordinate its context. It may not:
- own LegaKeys core;
- block or disable LegaKeys-managed Beat services;
- manufacture provider availability;
- grant system-wide authority;
- convert membership or role into authorization;
- bypass the common execution gate.

## Account model

A person's LegaKeys Account authenticates the person. Their Participant record represents participation. Workspace Membership represents scoped access to this operational environment. Workspace Role describes the function performed in the workspace. None of these independently authorizes consequential action.

## Operating loop

Observe → Understand → Queue → Prepare → Request/Propose → Authorization → Execute → Event → Evidence → Outcome.

## Truth states

CURRENT, UNKNOWN, UNAVAILABLE, PENDING, DENIED, FAILED, EXPIRED, REVOKED, COMPLETED.

## First-class modules

People & Participation; Places & Facilities; Work & Requests; Access Operations; Services; Governance & Evidence; Intelligence.

## AI boundary

GENESIS may observe, reason, detect and propose. CONSTANTYNA may explain, clarify and guide. Neither can grant authority or execute consequential actions without governed authorization.

**NO AUTHORIZATION → NO CONSEQUENTIAL ACTION.**
