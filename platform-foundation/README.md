# LEGAKEYS — PLATFORM FOUNDATION

## Purpose

The Platform Foundation is the mature, production-oriented foundation for turning the LegaKeys architecture into a coherent smart-platform product.

It is not a collection of disconnected apps.

It is one governed platform with:

- one identity and participation model
- one relationship/context model
- one capability model
- one authorization model
- one operational graph
- one Digital Twin
- one intelligence layer
- one service fabric
- one workspace model
- one evidence/history model
- one coherent experience

## Product direction

**LegaKeys**  
**Intelligent Living Infrastructure for Participating People and Communities**

> **Who you are. Where you Belong. One ecosystem.**

Built by **United of Balega**.

The Platform Foundation is designed to feel as mature as the strongest modern enterprise, cloud, smart-building, civic-tech and consumer platforms while remaining distinctly LegaKeys.

## Foundation principles

1. **One platform, many contexts.**
2. **Identity once; participation many times.**
3. **Context before action.**
4. **Capability does not imply authority.**
5. **Authorization gates every consequential action.**
6. **Truth states remain explicit.**
7. **Every consequential action creates an event and evidence.**
8. **The Digital Twin represents the known world without inventing certainty.**
9. **AI proposes; governed authority decides.**
10. **Services expose real capabilities and real connection state.**
11. **Community operations and LegaKeys operations remain separate.**
12. **The experience should be calm, fast, clear and spatially coherent.**

## The product model

LegaKeys should not present the user with seventeen architectural domains.

The user experiences a small number of durable surfaces:

- **Home** — what matters now
- **Places** — where the participant is connected
- **Services** — what can be requested or used
- **Activity** — what happened
- **Workspaces** — where the participant operates
- **Assistant** — conversation, discovery and guided action
- **Identity** — who the participant is
- **More** — administration, governance and advanced controls

The platform resolves the underlying architecture invisibly.

## Product quality bar

The foundation must meet the following bar before a surface is considered mature:

- no invented data
- no fake provider connections
- no fake availability
- no fake transaction success
- no ambiguous authorization
- no destructive action without confirmation where required
- no hidden context changes
- no silent identity duplication
- no inaccessible critical state
- no screen that depends on the user understanding internal architecture
- every important state has a visible truth/status representation
- every consequential operation is traceable to evidence
