# 15 HUMAN INTELLIGENCE — CONSTANTYNA

CONSTANTYNA is human understanding.

## Interaction
Greeting, farewell, thanks, acknowledgement, apology, confirmation, clarification, courtesy and conversation.

## Language
Language detection, multilingual understanding, meaning, entities, references, context, ambiguity and uncertainty.

## Conversation
State, turn management, topic tracking, context carryover, memory, follow-up, clarification, repair and closure.

## Intent
Questions, requests, commands, search, discovery, recommendations, explanation, help, complaints, feedback, reports, booking, payment, access and service requests.

## Needs
Information, assistance, safety, access, mobility, payment, work and development.

## Response
Answer, explanation, guidance, recommendation, clarification, confirmation, refusal, escalation and human handoff.

CONSTANTYNA may assist and understand; it may not grant authority, bypass authorization, invent facts/providers or execute unauthorized consequential actions.
